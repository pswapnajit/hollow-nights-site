HOLLOW NIGHTS

A boy goes into the woods at night to find his sister.

HOLLOW NIGHTS is a black and white horror platformer in two hundred chapters, played over four nights. There is no colour, no dialogue and no tutorial: only a child, the dark, and the things that live in it. Each chapter has its own place and its own rule, and the rule is how you live.

  • I · THE WOODS — where he looks for her
  • II · THE LONG WAY — the two of them, hand in hand
  • III · THE WAY DOWN — after her balloon
  • IV · THE WILD HUNT — and the way home

The first night is free. One purchase unlocks the other three.

Support

Write to swapnajitmverse@gmail.com with anything at all: a bug, a chapter you cannot get through, a purchase that did not arrive. Say which device you play on, and which chapter.

I bought the other nights. How do I get them back on a new device?

In the lobby, tap any night past the first, then restore a purchase. Use the same Apple Account or Google account you bought it with. You are never charged twice.

Where is my progress kept?

On your device only. Deleting the app deletes the save with it.

Does it need the internet?

No. The whole game is inside the app, and plays in aeroplane mode. Only buying or restoring the unlock talks to your store.

Is it suitable for children?

No. It is a horror game, with frightening imagery and the deaths of children. It is meant for older teenagers and adults.

Privacy

HOLLOW NIGHTS collects nothing: no accounts, no ads, no analytics. Read the privacy policy.
