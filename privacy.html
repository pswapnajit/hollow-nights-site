Privacy Policy

HOLLOW NIGHTS

Patra Labs  ·  Effective 23 November 2026

HOLLOW NIGHTS does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and it never contacts us. Everything the game needs is contained in the app itself.

This policy explains that in full, and what little data the game does keep on your own device.

1. Who we are

HOLLOW NIGHTS is developed and published by Patra Labs. If you have any question about this policy or about privacy in the game, write to swapnajitmverse@gmail.com and we will answer.

2. Information we collect

None.

We do not collect, receive, or have access to any information about you or your device. Specifically, the game does not collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact detail
  • your location, at any level of precision
  • device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or IP addresses
  • contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data
  • usage analytics, telemetry, crash reports, or diagnostic data

On iOS, the app requests no permissions at all. On Android, it declares one: Google Play’s billing permission, which lets it ask Google Play whether you own the unlock. It shows no prompt and gives the app no access to your data. There is nothing for us to see, because nothing ever leaves your device for us.

3. Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress so you can stop playing and come back to it. This is written to your device’s own storage and is never transmitted anywhere. It consists of:

  • which chapters you have unlocked and completed
  • your current chapter and checkpoint
  • counts of deaths and total play time
  • your best result for each chapter
  • your settings, such as brightness and whether screen shake is on

That is the whole of it. It contains nothing personal and nothing that identifies you. We cannot read it — it never leaves the device.

To delete it, delete the app. Uninstalling removes the saved game with it. There is nothing else to erase and no request you need to make of us, because we hold nothing.

4. Network connections

The game itself makes none. Every image, sound, chapter and model is packaged inside the app and loaded from local storage, and HOLLOW NIGHTS plays the same with your device in aeroplane mode. The only conversation with the outside world is the one your device’s own store has when you buy or restore the unlock (see section 6): it is between your device and Apple or Google, and it never reaches us.

5. Advertising and analytics

There is no advertising in HOLLOW NIGHTS and there are no advertising networks in it. There are no analytics, attribution, or crash-reporting services either. The only third-party code in the app is the platform’s own purchase library — Apple’s StoreKit on iOS, Google Play Billing on Android — used for the one purchase and nothing else.

We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not build any profile of you. There is no data to sell and we would not sell it.

6. Purchases

HOLLOW NIGHTS offers a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks the chapters beyond the first night. If you buy it, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple’s App Store or by Google Play, according to the platform you are on.

Those companies process the payment and tell the app only whether the purchase is valid. We never see and never receive your card details, billing address, or any other payment information. Apple’s and Google’s own privacy policies govern that transaction.

7. Children

HOLLOW NIGHTS is a horror game containing graphic and disturbing imagery. It is not designed for or directed at children, and it is rated accordingly on both stores.

We do not knowingly collect information from children — or from anyone else, since we collect no information at all.

8. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export, and delete the personal data a company holds about you.

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete. If you would like that confirmed in writing, write to swapnajitmverse@gmail.com and we will confirm it.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes what it does with data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date at the top will change. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes on the store.
